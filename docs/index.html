Privacy Policy for AITextBlur

Last updated: June 12, 2026

1. Overview

AITextBlur is an image editing tool powered by Google ML Kit for AI text detection and photo pixelation blur. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use and store user data, compliant with Google Play Policies, GDPR, CCPA, Japan APPI and Korea PIPA.

2. Data We Collect & How We Use It

2.1 Local Photos (Processed Locally Only, No Cloud Upload)

We access photos from your device gallery or shared images from other apps solely to perform text detection, mosaic editing and save edited pictures.

All image processing runs 100% offline on your device. Original images will never be uploaded to our servers. Images are only stored locally on your phone cache or gallery; all cache data will be erased once you uninstall the app. We have no access to your original photos.

2.2 Anonymous Device Information (Ads & Crash Analytics)

For free users only: we collect anonymous device ID, device model, OS version and crash logs to display banner ads and improve app stability. No personal identifiable information is tracked.

Paid Pro users will disable all ad-related data collection.

2.3 Offline Google ML Kit Text Recognition

Text detection is processed offline on your device via Google ML Kit. Detected text data only exists temporarily in memory and will be discarded after editing, no text content will be sent to our servers.

2.4 Payment Data (We Do NOT Collect Any Payment Info)

All in-app purchases are processed exclusively through Google Play Billing. We never collect, store or access your credit card, billing or Google account payment details. All payment transactions are governed by Google's privacy terms.

2.5 Local Trial Timer Data

The timestamp of your first app launch is saved locally only to calculate your 14-day full-feature trial period, no cloud synchronization.

3. Advertising Statement

Free trial & expired free versions display AdMob banner ads. Anonymous device identifiers are collected for ad delivery; users in EU/UK/Turkey can opt out of personalized ads.

One-time purchased Pro version removes all ads permanently, and stops all ad data collection.

4. Data Sharing

We will never sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties, except:

  • Official Google SDKs (ML Kit, AdMob, Google Billing), limited to anonymous device data required for SDK functions, subject to Google's own privacy policy.
  • Legal mandatory requests from official judicial authorities.

5. User Rights

  • Delete data: Uninstall the app to clear all local cache and trial records; gallery photos are fully controlled by you.
  • Revoke permissions: Disable storage/gallery permission in your system settings to stop photo import & export.
  • EU/California residents: You may contact us to request access or deletion of all anonymous usage logs via our support email.

6. Children's Policy

This app is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this policy, updates will be notified inside the app. Continued use of the app means you accept the revised policy.
